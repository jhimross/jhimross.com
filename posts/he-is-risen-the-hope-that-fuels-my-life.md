Today, I pause to reflect on the greatest truth that changed everything , "**Jesus is alive**!"

The resurrection is not just a moment in history I believe in. It’s the foundation of my everyday life.  
Jesus Christ who was crucified, buried, and seemed defeated rose again. The tomb is empty. Death lost. Hope won.

![](https://jhimross.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/0e63f-2020-04-13.jpg?w=1024&h=718)

Because Jesus lives, I live with purpose.  
When I bike through the streets chasing sunsets, when I share a simple cup of black coffee, when I open my Bible and lead others in prayer it’s not just routine. It’s all part of walking with a living Savior who loves me, walks with me, and never leaves me.

There are days when life feels uncertain. Prayers seem delayed, or waiting becomes hard.  
But the resurrection reminds me: **God always finishes what He starts.**  
The cross looked like defeat, but it was victory. The grave looked final, but it was just the beginning.

Because He rose:

  * My past is forgiven.
  * My present is filled with His strength.
  * My future is secure.



**"He is not here; He has risen, just as He said."** (Matthew 28:6)

This is the hope that fuels my life in the good days, in the hard days, and in every ordinary moment in between.  
Jesus is alive, and because of that, I have everything I need.

![](http://jhimross.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/PXL_20240329_175228301-1024x576.jpg)

**He is risen! He is risen indeed!**
